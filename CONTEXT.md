# Mamma Pizza Menu Browser

A demo menu experience for the article "Practical Spec Driven Development from Frontend Engineers". It presents an imaginary Italian family trattoria to readers and colleagues through an Instagram Stories-like browsing flow.

## Experience

**Menu Browser**:
A browse-only experience for exploring the restaurant's menu. It has no customer accounts, ordering, payment, or staff workflows.
_Avoid_: Ordering app, restaurant management system

**Story**:
A full-screen presentation in the browsing flow, representing one menu item or a short menu message. Stories can be advanced manually and can also progress automatically.
_Avoid_: Post, slide, page

**Story Sequence**:
The ordered set of three Stories belonging to one Category. Readers can browse a sequence manually or let it progress automatically.
_Avoid_: Feed, carousel, playlist

**Story Frame**:
The portrait viewing area in which a Story is presented. It is a phone-like frame on desktop and fills the screen on mobile.
_Avoid_: Screen, viewport, card

**Progress Timer**:
The visual indication of the current Story's position and automatic progression through its Story Sequence.
_Avoid_: Loading bar, countdown

**Playback Control**:
A small circular control in the top-right corner of the Story Frame that pauses or resumes automatic Story progression. It remains available on the final Story to replay the sequence.
_Avoid_: Menu button, action button, settings

**Story Circle**:
A circular Instagram-style Category Navigation marker containing an emoji and its Category label.
_Avoid_: Avatar, thumbnail, icon button

**Menu Item**:
A dish or drink offered by Mamma Pizza!, with a name, description, ingredients, price, nutrition facts, and category.
_Avoid_: Product, order line

**Category**:
A named grouping of menu items used to browse the menu: Pizza, Antipasti, Pasta, Insalate, Dolci, or Drinks.
_Avoid_: Department, collection

**Featured Dish**:
The first menu item presented when the Menu Browser opens: Margherita in the Pizza Category. It is a dish from the menu, not a separate promotional or ordering concept.
_Avoid_: Hero product, special offer

**Category Navigation**:
The way a Reader chooses which Category's Story Sequence to browse. Categories appear as circular Instagram-style markers above the Story Frame, keeping the Menu Browser focused on exploration rather than ordering.
_Avoid_: Main menu, filter, tab bar

**Nutrition Facts**:
The informational nutrition summary shown for a Menu Item per serving: calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. It does not create dietary, medical, or ordering workflows.
_Avoid_: Dietary label, health claim, allergen warning

**Palette**:
The restrained Mamma Pizza! color system: warm paper as the dominant color, deep olive as the structural color, tomato red as the accent, and charcoal for readable text.
_Avoid_: Theme, gradient, color scheme

## People and Place

**Mamma Pizza!**:
The imaginary Italian family trattoria represented by the demo. Its voice and visual character are rustic, warm, and family-oriented.
_Avoid_: Brand system, real restaurant

**Reader**:
A person viewing the demo, including the article author, a small number of readers, or colleagues from the author's organization.
_Avoid_: Customer, staff member

**Trattoria Voice**:
The warm, rustic, family-oriented language used to describe Mamma Pizza! and its Menu Items.
_Avoid_: Marketing copy, brand voice
